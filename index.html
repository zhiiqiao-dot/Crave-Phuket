About Us

Located in the beautiful Bang Tao Beach, Phuket, Crave Restaurant and Bar offers a unique dining experience with a blend of international cuisines and local flavors.

Our philosophy is simple: fresh ingredients, creative recipes, and a relaxed atmosphere where everyone can enjoy delicious food and good company.

Whether you're looking for a hearty breakfast, a quick lunch, or a romantic dinner with beach views, Crave has something for everyone.
